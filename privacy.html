Your privacy

Privacy policy

Elin Wolf is responsible for the personal information handled by this business. This policy describes how information collected through elinwolf.com is used. For privacy questions, contact music.by.elink@gmail.com.

Event inquiries and email records

When you inquire, you provide your name, email address, message and any event date, type or location you choose to share. These details are used to respond, discuss availability and plan your performance.

The form sends your information to FormSubmit, which processes the submission and forwards it to Elin’s Gmail inbox. FormSubmit is contacted when you submit, whether or not you accept Analytics. Please avoid including sensitive personal information. See FormSubmit’s privacy information.

Client inquiries and booking correspondence are retained in Gmail for business records, future bookings and returning clients. There is no fixed deletion period. You can contact Elin to request correction or deletion; applicable recordkeeping needs will be considered. Gmail is provided by Google under its privacy policy.

Analytics and advertising attribution

Google Analytics 4 is optional. Until you accept Analytics, this website does not load the Analytics tag or send Analytics events. With acceptance, Analytics uses cookies and receives information about visits, pages, browsers, devices and referral or campaign information to help Elin understand how people find and use the site.

If Analytics is accepted and your consent choice stays unchanged from the start of an inquiry submission through its successful confirmation, the website sends an inquiry event and an event category, such as wedding or proposal. That event does not include your name, email address, venue, date or message. Inquiries made without consent are not saved for later Analytics reporting.

Advertising measurement is a separate, optional choice alongside Analytics. If you select it and accept, Google may store advertising cookies and use visit, ad-click and successful-inquiry data with Elin’s linked Google Ads account to attribute inquiries to ads and measure advertising performance. Analytics acceptance alone does not grant this permission. Without advertising-measurement consent, click-based Ads attribution is limited. Personalized advertising, remarketing and Google Signals remain disabled regardless of your choices. This website does not add a separate Google Ads tag or enhanced conversions. Learn how Google uses information from sites that use its services.

The current Analytics property retains event-level data for two months and user data for fourteen months, with the user-data period reset on new activity. These controls do not determine how long aggregated reports are retained. Gmail correspondence is retained separately as described above.

Hosting, fonts and media

GitHub Pages hosts this website. GitHub receives technical request information, such as IP addresses, when serving pages and files. See GitHub’s privacy statement.

The site also requests fonts from Google Fonts and icon styles from jsDelivr to display its design. These providers receive technical connection information when those resources load, independently of Analytics consent. See Google Fonts’ privacy information and jsDelivr’s privacy policy.

Video previews are hosted on this website and link to YouTube; there are no automatically loaded YouTube or Instagram players. Following a YouTube or Instagram link opens that provider’s service, where its own privacy practices apply. See Google and YouTube’s privacy policy and Instagram’s privacy policy.

Your choices

Choose Accept selected to allow Analytics. Advertising measurement is included only if you also select its optional checkbox. Choose Reject all to decline both. You can keep browsing and submit an inquiry without accepting. Use Privacy choices in the footer at any time: uncheck advertising measurement and choose Accept selected to withdraw that purpose while retaining Analytics, or choose Reject all to withdraw both. Changing an active choice stops the previous Analytics runtime and discards its unsent queued activity. Withdrawing advertising measurement clears accessible first-party advertising measurement cookies; rejecting both also clears accessible first-party Analytics cookies. Your page and inquiry text are preserved. Accepting again enables new measurement; discarded activity is not replayed. Withdrawal cannot recall requests already sent or delete data already received by Google.

We store only your Analytics and advertising-measurement choices in this browser’s local storage so it can apply across pages and visits. Clearing site storage resets the choice. If your browser blocks storage, the choice applies only to the current page. Personalized advertising remains off in every case. With JavaScript disabled, Analytics and advertising measurement stay off.

You can also manage cookies in your browser, use Google’s Analytics opt-out browser add-on, or email Elin directly instead of using the form.
