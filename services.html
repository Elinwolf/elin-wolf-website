02 · Corporate Events

Corporate Event Violinist in Los Angeles

Polished entertainment that reflects your event.

Elin Wolf provides live violin for corporate events in Los Angeles and throughout Southern California, from cocktail receptions and gala dinners to brand activations and holiday celebrations. Music is tailored to the setting, with sophisticated live music for receptions and dining or a featured performance for a key moment.

Elin coordinates with event planners, DJs and production teams on musical selections, performance cues, setup and transitions. Professional sound equipment is available, with venue-system and AV coordination arranged in advance to suit the space and event schedule.

Ideal forCocktail receptions, galas, corporate dinners, brand launches and activations, conferences, awards events and holiday parties
FormatsGuest-arrival music, ambient reception and dinner sets, featured performances and carefully timed musical cues
ProductionAdvance coordination with planners, DJs and AV teams, including professional sound, venue requirements and the event’s running order
